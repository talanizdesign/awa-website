All your feelings count.

Log the apps that bring you joy or calm, and the ones that leave you tired or restless. Knowing both helps you decide.

Log when you feel like it.

No reminders, no streaks. You open Awa when an app made you feel something. Awa shows you how you feel, and what you do with your apps is always up to you.

What you log is yours.

  • Only you can see your logs.
  • Awa never sees inside your apps. You choose what to log and when.
  • Your data is stored in the EU.
  • Delete your account whenever you want, and your logs go with it.

Questions

Is Awa free?

Yes, Awa is free to download.

Do I have to log every time I use an app?

No. Log when you feel like it, good feelings and bad ones.

Which apps can I log?

Any app from the App Store, including the ones you pay for.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Awa is for iPhone first.
